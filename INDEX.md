# Arbol Root: Catalog

- [`contract/`](contract/README.md): the Root Contract (`RCT`): what every Trunk of a Root does and what the Root gives every Trunk, version 1: the command `arbol-trunk`, the folder of an Exchange, starting, stopping and settling, the chat tables as `layout.sql`, and the conformance suite.
- [`specs/`](specs/README.md): the Root manager `arbol-root` (`RTM`): its commands, and what its Builds carry: the table layouts and the migration script of each Root.

## Conventions

- Every artifact goes into a chapter folder, never directly into the corpus root. `README.md`, `INDEX.md` and a `GLOSSARY.md` are the exceptions.
- List a chapter here when it is created.
